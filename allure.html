About this Project

Allure is a residential real estate project developed by Entremares in Tijuana, Mexico. The project consists of 2 premium townhouses designed for the mid-to-high end market. Construction began in January 2023 and was completed ahead of schedule in December 2024, delivering a 22% net return to investors.

Project Highlights
  • Strong developer track record with Entremares
  • Premium location in Tijuana with high demand
  • Delivered on time and within budget
  • 22% net return achieved, above the 18–20% target
