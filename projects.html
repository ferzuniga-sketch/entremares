Kabil

Kabil was Entremares' first project — a four-unit luxury apartment development in Tijuana's emerging premium residential corridor. From groundbreaking to fully sold, Kabil delivered on every dimension: on budget, on schedule, and above return expectations.

Each apartment was designed with an emphasis on high-end finishes, spatial efficiency, and natural light — a signature of AEDIFIKA's residential philosophy. The project sold out in just 3 months from first listing, validating both the product quality and the market positioning.
